2. What data may be processed?

The Bot processes Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames or display names, applicable role information, experience points (XP), levels, message counts, server configuration, and information needed to operate XP codes, prizes, game participation, and administrative XP records.

The Bot may read the text of ordinary messages where needed to recognize XP redemption codes, riddle solutions and numeric guesses in interactive games. Messages and related event data may also be processed to calculate XP and apply cooldowns.

3. Why is data used?

  • Maintain persistent XP, levels, rankings and progression.
  • Assign and synchronize level-based Discord roles.
  • Process chat-based codes, riddles, number games, drops and giveaways.
  • Manage server settings and review administrative changes to XP.
  • Prevent repeat rewards within configured cooldown periods and keep the Bot operational.

4. Legal basis

The Bot processes information to support server-level progression, role rewards and voluntary use of its interactive features. Where applicable, this processing is based on legitimate interests in operating these community features, subject to applicable law and the rights of individuals.

5. Storage and retention

XP, levels, message counts, settings and related activity records may be stored in the Bot database while needed to provide server features. Records can remain after a member leaves a server until they are deleted; contact the operator to request deletion. Text of chat messages is accessed where needed for games and redemption codes. Retention of any data handled by optional modules depends on the active features and configuration.

6. Sharing and security

The Bot works through the Discord platform and the systems used to host the Bot and its database. The operator does not sell Discord member data to advertisers. Stored information should be accessible only to authorized operators, with appropriate protective measures.
